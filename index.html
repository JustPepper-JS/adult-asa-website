NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Which cluster should I choose?

Legacy emphasizes convenience and quality-of-life tools. Limited emphasizes exploration and slower progression. Your choice depends on how you want to play.

Are there server wipes?

Adult ASA advertises no scheduled full wipes. The community has an inactivity policy, so old or inactive bases may still be removed. See Discord for current rules.

Where can I find the mod list?

Visit the mod research library. Its published entries clearly distinguish historical research from recently observed enabled-mod lists; a real-time installation inventory is still being developed.

How fresh are the player counts?

The servers page shows the source timestamp and connection status. Missing or stale data appears as unavailable, not as a made-up player count.
