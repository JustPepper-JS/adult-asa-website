PLAYER SUPPORT

Lost something?
Tell us what happened.

ARK has its moments. If a transfer, crash, or other issue cost you your gear, send us the details. We'll check what we can verify and let you know what happens next.

Request item recovery

You don't need to be staff to submit a request. Please only send one report for the same incident.

What happens next?

  1. We check your report. Staff will look into the incident and any available saves.
  2. A senior staff member reviews replacements. Moderators can't approve their own requests. Staff approvals require two-factor sign-in.
  3. We arrange delivery. If replacement is approved and available, we'll contact you to get the items back to your survivor.

Old saves help us investigate, but they don't always show exactly what was lost. Modded items and stored creatures may take a separate check.
